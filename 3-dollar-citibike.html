$3
CITI BIKE

A 45-minute ride shouldn't cost more than $3.

Citi Bike is too expensive. Electric Citi Bike rides have tripled in price in just a few years — and what used to be an accessible way to get around is becoming increasingly out of reach for many New Yorkers. A 45-minute bike ride shouldn't cost more than $3 — and we're bringing Citi Bike riders together to form the City Bike Riders Union, calling for public subsidies and efficiency improvements to bring the price down.
